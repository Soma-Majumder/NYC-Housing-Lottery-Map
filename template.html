    How to apply

    1. Make a free account on NYC Housing Connect.
    2. Fill in your household profile: everyone who will live with you and all household income.
    3. Check the listing for that building's income limits and household sizes. Each lottery has its own.
    4. Apply online before the deadline. One application per household per lottery. Applying is always free.
    5. Wait to be contacted. If your application is picked, the building will ask for documents like pay stubs, tax returns and ID.

    Never pay anyone to apply or to get a better spot. Free help filling out applications is available through HPD's Housing Ambassadors program. Deadlines shown come from HPD's dataset; confirm on Housing Connect.
